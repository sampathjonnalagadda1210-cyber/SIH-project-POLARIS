GOVERNMENT OF INDIA — MODEL TENDER DOCUMENT (DEMO)
Public Works Department — Circular No. PW/RCC/2026/114
Subject: Rate contract for supply of construction materials, FY 2026-27

1. SCOPE OF SUPPLY
The successful bidder shall supply and deliver the following materials to
departmental stores and work sites as per the delivery schedule:

   (a) High tensile strength deformed steel bars (TMT), grade Fe 500D,
       for reinforcement of reinforced cement concrete (RCC) works,
       conforming to IS 1786:2008.

   (b) Ordinary Portland Cement, 33 grade, conforming to IS 269, in
       50 kg bags with BIS certification mark printed on each bag.

   (c) Coarse and fine aggregates from natural sources for concrete,
       conforming to IS 383:2016.

   (d) Ready mixed concrete of grade M25 for bridge works, produced and
       transported as per IS 4926.

2. TESTING REQUIREMENTS
All materials shall be tested by the manufacturer at BIS-recognized
laboratories. Tensile testing of steel bars and chemical analysis shall be
carried out as per the applicable Indian Standards. Compressive strength
tests on concrete cubes shall be arranged at every delivery batch.

3. COMPLIANCE
The bidders must possess valid BIS certification licences for all products
covered under mandatory certification. Consignments without valid
certification marks shall be rejected. Steel and cement shall comply with
the current edition of the relevant standard together with all amendments
published up to the date of the tender.

4. DELIVERY AND WARRANTY
Materials shall be delivered within 30 days of purchase order. Any
non-conforming material shall be replaced free of cost within 15 days of
detection of defect.
