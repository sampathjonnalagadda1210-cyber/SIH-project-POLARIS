# Abstract — SIH 2026 Idea Submission

**Problem Statement ID:** SIH26108 — AI-Powered Recommendation Engine for Identifying Applicable Indian Standards for Procurement Specifications
**Theme:** Smart Automation · **Category:** Software
**Solution Name:** POLARIS (Procurement-Oriented Listing of Applicable References in Indian Standards) — by Team ATLAS

---

Government departments, Public Sector Enterprises and private organizations procure goods through tenders whose technical specifications must reference the correct Indian Standards (IS). With thousands of published standards, overlapping scopes and frequent revisions, officials routinely omit relevant standards, cite superseded editions, or miss the test-method, terminology and safety standards that each cited standard normatively requires — leading to ambiguity, quality failures and procurement disputes.

**POLARIS** is an AI-powered recommendation engine that turns any product description, technical specification or tender document into a complete, current and certification-aware standards package in seconds. Users provide input four ways — typing or pasting text in English, Hindi (Devanagari) or Hinglish; uploading .txt/.pdf tender documents (with CID/Identity-H font decoding for real government PDFs); photographing a document page (in-browser OCR, no cloud); or speaking. The engine then (1) recommends the most applicable primary Indian Standards using semantic understanding rather than keyword matching; (2) traverses each recommended standard's normative references as a graph to surface the allied standards a tender must also cite — test methods, terminology, safety and installation codes; (3) verifies every cited edition against the latest published version and lists pending amendments; (4) maps mandatory certification requirements (BIS ISI Mark, CRS) onto the recommendations; (5) computes a Specification Health Score with an explicit gap list quantifying what the specification is missing; (6) explains every recommendation through a conversational assistant whose answers carry IS-code citations and honest refusals when data is insufficient; and (7) produces a printable assessment report plus a ready-to-paste specification block for insertion into GeM and e-procurement workflows.

**What is novel:** the normative-reference graph traversal (computing the complete set of allied standards a specification implies, visualized as an interactive Standards Map) is absent from existing keyword-search tools; the Specification Health Score converts tender quality into a single verifiable number; four-mode document ingestion with zero cloud dependency; and concept-level multilingual mapping lets the Hindi word "सरिया" retrieve the same precise result as "reinforcement steel bars" — semantic matching demonstrated, not claimed. **Measured accuracy:** 96% top-1 and 100% top-3 on a published 25-case ground-truth benchmark (`bench_accuracy.py`), validated end-to-end on real government tender PDFs, including a 202-page port-authority tender.

**Feasibility:** a working prototype exists today — a tabbed web app (Analyze / Standards Map / Assistant / Library / Report), a Python REST API, and a structured corpus of 69 curated standards across 16 procurement categories with editions, amendments, certification status and cross-reference edges. It runs fully offline on any laptop with zero installations; the corpus schema mirrors the BIS catalog, so full-catalog ingest is a data task, not a redesign. The semantic layer is modular, upgradeable to multilingual embedding models in production.

**Impact:** specification drafting shrinks from hours to seconds; complete, current citations reduce tender ambiguity, bid disputes and re-tender cycles; MSMEs and startups face fairer tenders and fewer disqualification surprises; mandatory-certification flags keep non-compliant products out of public procurement — directly advancing the quality-infrastructure goals of the Department of Consumer Affairs and BIS, supporting Digital India and Make in India with measurable improvement in the standards-compliance of public procurement.
